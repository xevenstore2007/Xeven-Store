See It In Action

See the Difference: Before and After

A real example from the book, using prompt 2.01.

The prompt

Write a Shopify product description for [product] aimed at [buyer]. Real specs and facts: [specs]. Structure: one-line hook, 3 benefit bullets (benefit first, feature second), a short specs list, one closing line. Do not invent features, materials or certifications. Under 150 words. Return HTML using only <p>, <ul> and <li> tags.

Before: supplier text

Ceramic coffee mug. 12oz. Dishwasher safe. Microwave safe. Best quality! Perfect gift for everyone. Must have item!!!

After: prompt 2.01

Your morning coffee, in a mug that feels good to hold.

  • Pour a full 12 oz and skip the constant refills.
  • Clean up fast: it is dishwasher safe.
  • Reheat in seconds: it is microwave safe.

Specs: ceramic, 12 oz, dishwasher safe, microwave safe.

Pick one up and make tomorrow's coffee a little better.

Example 2 · Prompt 5.13

Write abandoned cart email 1, sent 1 hour after, helpful and not pushy. Remind them what they left, include shipping and returns facts [policy], and offer help with questions. Use placeholders {{first_name}}, {{product_name}}, {{cart_link}}. Under 90 words. Give a subject under 50 characters and preview text.

Before

Hi, you forgot something! Hurry, items sell out fast! Buy now before it is gone!!!

After

Subject: Still thinking about your mug?
Preview: Your cart is saved, and help is one reply away.

Hi {{first_name}}, you left {{product_name}} in your cart, and it is still saved for you: {{cart_link}}

Orders ship in 2 business days, and returns are accepted within 30 days. If you have a question, just reply to this email and we will help.

What changed: False urgency removed. Real shipping and returns facts added (supplied as [policy]). One clear link and an offer of help.

Example 3 · Prompt 6.06

Write a reply to a customer asking where order {{order_number}} is. Status: [status]. Tracking: {{tracking_link}}. Real estimated delivery: [estimate]. Include what I'll do if it doesn't arrive by [date]. No invented dates. Under 110 words.

Before

Customer message: “Hi, I ordered a week ago and nothing has come. Where is my order?? Please help.”

Details given to the prompt: status in transit, estimated delivery October 9, follow-up date October 12.

After

Hi {{first_name}}, thank you for your patience, and sorry for the wait. Order {{order_number}} is in transit with the carrier. You can follow it here: {{tracking_link}}

The estimated delivery date is October 9. If it has not arrived by October 12, reply here and I will contact the carrier and update you within one business day.

What changed: A calm, specific answer with a real date and a clear next step. No invented dates.

A writing example, not a sales result. Your outcomes depend on your product, market, and effort.
