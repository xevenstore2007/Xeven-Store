14-day refund window

Key point

If the toolkit is not what you expected, you can ask for a refund within 14 days of purchase. No long forms and no hoops to jump through.

How to request a refund

Email support@xeven.store within 14 days of purchase and include:

  • Your order number
  • The email address you used at checkout

We will confirm by email once your request is approved.

How you get paid back

We refund the purchase price to your original payment method. Once the refund is issued, the time it takes to appear depends on your bank or card provider. Payments are processed by our checkout provider, 2Checkout.

Please note

  • Requests made after the 14-day window are not covered.
  • The toolkit is a digital download, so nothing is shipped or returned.
  • We do not guarantee sales or rankings, so results alone are not a basis for a refund outside the 14-day window.

Need a refund?

We usually reply by email.
